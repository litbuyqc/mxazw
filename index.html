03 / An Oopbuy shopping guide

From interesting find
to informed choice.

A product link is a starting point. These four checks make a shortlist useful without assuming that a spreadsheet entry is complete or current.

01

Choose the category

Begin with the item you need. Shoes, jackets and accessories call for different comparisons, so keep each shortlist focused.

02

Record the exact variant

Save the listing, selected color, size and included items. Similar pictures can represent different materials or configurations.

03

Check the evidence

Read measurements and seller information. If inspection photos are available, compare them with the variant you selected.

04

Confirm the full cost

Review the checkout total and relevant shipping, service and return terms. Recheck details when you are ready to pay.

04 / The comparison desk

Keep the details
that change the decision.

Use this checklist alongside any Oopbuy product list. Missing information is a reason to ask a question before buying.

A practical checklist for your shopping shortlist
Product type Details to record Question to resolve
Shoes Size system, recommended foot length, upper material, sole and colorway. Does the listing explain how its sizing relates to your foot measurements?
Hoodies & T-shirts Chest width, length, shoulder width, fabric composition and fit. How do the garment measurements compare with a piece you already wear?
Jackets & sets Layering room, lining, closure, top and bottom measurements, included pieces. Is the price for the full set or a single item?
Pants & shorts Waist, rise, inseam, stretch and leg opening. Are the dimensions body measurements or measurements of the garment?
Headwear & jewelry Circumference, adjustability, dimensions, base material and fastening. Will it fit, and are its materials and care requirements clearly stated?
Underwear & jerseys Size chart, fabric, selected style, personalization and return conditions. Do hygiene or customization conditions affect your return options?

Budget for the whole order: product price + any domestic delivery + service charges + international shipping + applicable taxes or duties. Confirm which costs are included; a spreadsheet price alone is not a final quotation.

06 / Clear questions, useful answers

Before you
open another tab.

The essentials behind the directory, the artwork and your next shopping decision.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-reference list used to organize shopping links and compare details such as category, size, listed price and seller information. The information included varies by its creator.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and a comparison guide. Its shopping links open CAFANS pages.

How should I compare shoes and clothing?

For shoes, compare the size chart, foot-length guidance, materials and exact colorway. For clothing, compare garment measurements, fabric composition and fit notes with an item you already own.

Do product pictures prove authenticity?

No. Pictures and brand names alone do not establish authenticity. Review seller identity, purchase documentation and applicable return terms. This page’s brand-inspired illustrations are editorial artwork, not product photographs.

Does a listed price include international delivery?

Do not assume so. Confirm product price, domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties before payment.

Is this an official Oopbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent shopping guide with links to CAFANS. It is not an official Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès website.
